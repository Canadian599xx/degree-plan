## Key Takeaways
1. **Semesters 4–5 are your heaviest CS-major terms** (5 classes each). Spring 2027 also fully clears Life & Physical Sciences via GEO 1013 + GEO 1033. Your concentration sequence is: CS 3433 (Fa27) → CS 4353 & CS 4653 (Sp28).

4. **Semester 7 drops to just 1 class.** Consider pulling an elective forward from Semester 6 (or even 5) to smooth out your workload rather than coasting that hard in your second-to-last semester.
5. **Verify prereq assumptions with your advisor** before registering — I inferred prereqs from DegreeWorks notes, not the full catalog.
