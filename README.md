## ⚠️ Semester-Locked Courses — Critical Path

These are the courses in your plan that the catalog lists as offered in **only one term**. Everything else (CLEP exams, free electives, F/Sp/Su courses) has flexibility to shift a semester if needed — these do not. Missing one of these pushes your whole downstream sequence back by a full year, not a semester, since you'd be waiting for that term to come around again.

| Course | Only Offered | Must Happen | Why It's Critical |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CS 3433** | Fall | **Fall 2027 (Sem 5)** | Gates both CS 4353 and CS 4653 — the entire rest of your concentration depends on this one class landing on time. |
| **CS 4353** | Spring | Spring 2028 (Sem 6) | Needs CS 3433 done first; this is the very next Spring after Fall 2027. |
| **CS 4653** | Spring | Spring 2028 (Sem 6) | Same dependency as above. |
| **CS 3873** | Spring | Spring 2028 (Sem 6) | Gates CS 4453 & CS 4643 if you want those as bonus electives in Sem 8. |
| **CS 4683** | Spring | Spring 2028 (Sem 6) | No downstream dependents, but only offered Spring — can't shift to Fall 2028 if missed. |
| **CS 4823** | Fall | Fall 2028 (Sem 7) | No downstream dependents, but only offered Fall — can't shift to Spring 2029 without extending. |

**The one that actually matters most: CS 3433 in Fall 2027.** If you don't get a seat, or fail/withdraw, you're waiting until Fall 2028 to retake it — which then pushes CS 4353 and CS 4653 to Spring 2029 (your planned last semester), and you'd graduate in 9 semesters instead of 8, not 8.

**Practical advice:**
- **Register for CS 3433 the moment registration opens** for Fall 2027 — treat it like a priority registration, not a "get to it eventually" class.
- Everything else in the "locked" list has one built-in semester of slack (Sem 6 and Sem 7 both have room below 5 classes), so a single slip elsewhere is recoverable — CS 3433 slipping is the only one that isn't.
- **Double-check GEO 1033's offering term** with your advisor or the course catalog before registering for Spring 2027 — I don't have confirmed data on Geology department offering patterns, only the CS catalog.

## Key Takeaways
1. **CS 3433 has to happen in Fall 2027** — it's Fall-only in the catalog, and it's the gate for both remaining concentration courses. This is the one hard scheduling constraint in the whole plan; don't let it slip.
2. **Semesters 4 and 5 carry the heaviest load (5 classes each).** Semester 6 drops to 4 once concentration is finished, and Semester 7 drops to 3.
3. **All core requirements finish by Summer 2027** via GEO 1013/1033 plus three CLEP exams (HUM 2023, HIS 1053, ENG 2013).
4. **All coursework — major, concentration, electives, and free electives — is done by the end of Fall 2028.** Semester 8 is completely open.
5. **Double-check actual course scheduling each term with your advisor** — "generally offered" terms can shift, and a couple of the electives I picked (CS 4823, CS 3873, CS 4683) only run in one specific term, so there's limited room to shuffle if a specific section doesn't run when expected.
